The Question Changes Everything

❌ Lazy Prompt
"Write me a short paragraph about my weekend."
AI writes for you:"This weekend was a blast! I went to the park with my friends and we ran around for hours. Sunday was all about relaxing — I watched a movie and fell asleep on the couch."
AI does all the work. You learn nothing.
✅ Smart Prompt
"Here's my paragraph… Can you give me tips to improve it without rewriting it for me?"
AI gives you tips:"Add feelings — instead of 'fun,' tell us how it felt. Add one detail about the zoo: what did you see? Pick just one of these and try adding it!"
You stay in control. AI becomes your editor. You get smarter.
Golden Rule: Show your work first — then tell AI what kind of help you want.

⚡ Power Prompts

These work on any AI — ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, or Claude

🔍 Prompt 1
Gap Finder
Find out what you're missing
"I think [X] because [reason]. What am I missing or getting wrong?"
✏️ Prompt 2
Improver
Get better at something you made
"Here's my work: [paste it]. How could I improve it? Don't rewrite it — just tell me what to change and why."
⚠️ Always say "don't rewrite it" — or AI will just do it for you!
🧠 Prompt 3
Quiz Master
Check you really understood it
"Explain [topic] simply. Then give me 3 questions to test if I understood it."

Your Turn! 🚀

🔍 Round 1  ·  3 min
Gap Finder
Write one sentence about something you're learning. Use the Gap Finder prompt. What did AI say was missing?
"I think [X] because [reason]. What am I missing or getting wrong?"
e.g."I think dingoes are just wild dogs that escaped into the bush. What am I missing or getting wrong?"
✏️ Round 2  ·  3 min
Improver
Write a sentence about anything you like. Use the Improver prompt. Pick one suggestion and actually fix it.
"Here's my work: [paste it]. How could I improve it? Don't rewrite it — just tell me what to change and why."
e.g."Here's my paragraph: 'Dogs are great pets. They like to play.' How could I improve it? Don't rewrite it — just tell me what to change and why."
🧠 Round 3  ·  3 min
Quiz Master
Pick something you want to understand better. Use Quiz Master. Try to answer the 3 questions without looking anything up!
"Explain [topic] simply. Then give me 3 questions to test if I understood it."
e.g."Explain how dingoes are different from pet dogs simply. Then give me 3 questions to test if I understood it."
Finished early? Pick any prompt and try a different topic!

What You've Learned Today 🌟

💡
Show Your Thinking First
Don't ask for the answer — show your work and ask where you went wrong.
⚡
Use the Power Prompts
Gap Finder, Improver, and Quiz Master. Always add "don't rewrite it."
🤨
Watch Out for Hallucinations
AI can make up facts and sound very sure. Always check against another source.
🧠
Think for Yourself
AI can have hidden unfair assumptions. Your thinking always matters.
"The smartest people aren't the ones who know the most — they're the ones best at finding their own gaps."
